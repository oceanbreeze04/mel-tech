Perspective

My approach to AI

How I Think

I see AI as a powerful tool, not an unquestionable authority. Its value depends on how well it understands people, responds to context, and produces information that is genuinely useful.

01

Useful over impressive

A polished response means little if it misses the user’s intent, avoids the real question, or provides unreliable information.

02

Curiosity with a critical eye

I’m excited by what AI can do, but I also look closely at where it struggles, including ambiguity, bias, inconsistency, safety, and unusual edge cases.

03

Human judgment still matters

Strong AI systems are shaped by people who can recognize nuance, question weak outputs, and explain why something does or does not work. That is the perspective I bring to AI evaluation.
